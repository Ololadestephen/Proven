    Keep the trust boundaries visible

    Evidence has limits.

    Only the escrow’s private callback can record a verdict. A worker’s claim of success does not decide payment.

    • The OutLayer contract and its operator remain trusted, along with registered worker keys and approved measurements. A per-call quote is not verified on chain.
    • The relay can read or withhold private inputs. It cannot set a verdict.
    • The holder of the escrow’s full-access key can change its code.
    • Independent Intel signature verification is not performed by this page.
    • This viewer relies on the public testnet RPC and OutLayer API. It does not verify a NEAR light-client proof.
